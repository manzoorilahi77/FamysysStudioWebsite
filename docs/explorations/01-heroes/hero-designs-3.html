Famysys Studio

Ten heroes, ten living backgrounds, one shared frame loop.

Following 09 Flux. Every hero here carries a background of its own that is drawn, not placed — particles, cells, refraction, light, scanlines, mesh, gradient, trails, ink, and a distance field — and every one of them answers to the scroll and to the cursor. The copy is identical throughout and the palette is the same twelve. Five navigations vary the centred wordmark with split links; five do something else. Each design shows the work a different way.

SignalA signed-distance field — three shapes folding through one another in GLSL, centred wherever the pointer is. Wordmark left, links right, and a hairline beneath the bar that fills as the hero is scrolled. The column of plates never stops moving; scrolling speeds it up and reverses it, and the arrow says which way it is running.
