Ten heroes, ten different arguments for the same page.

Every one carries identical copy and draws on the same twelve colours. What changes is the composition, the weight of the type, where the logo and navigation sit, and — most of all — how the thing moves. Scroll through. Each has room beneath it so its scroll behaviour has somewhere to happen.
